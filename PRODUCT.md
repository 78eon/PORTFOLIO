## Platform
web

## Purpose and audience
A personal cybersecurity student portfolio for readers exploring practical lab work, CTF writeups, vulnerability research and learning notes. Success means finding and understanding real research, then following the author's GitHub or making contact. Audience emphasis is inferred from the supplied portfolio brief; no employment or professional expertise is claimed.

## Capabilities and constraints
Next.js 16 Pages Router, React 19, JavaScript, Tailwind 4 and PostgreSQL. Public content uses published-only queries and 60-second ISR. Notes preserve Markdown, headings and reading time. Writeups preserve evidence, severity, tools and references. Certifications come from the database. Contact requires explicit consent. Admin authentication, CRUD, Markdown editing and Cloudinary uploads remain operational contracts. AWS SDK is installed but current storage implementation uses Cloudinary.

## Brand and evidence
NEON; GitHub https://github.com/78eon. Database records are the authority for research and credentials. No projects dataset exists, so recent research supplies the featured section. Do not invent research titles, accomplishments, expertise, social profiles or availability. Existing privacy text remains intact.

## Principles
Evidence before claims. Readable research before decorative effects. Native links and resilient forms. Preserve backend and schema. No deployment or main merge in this task.
